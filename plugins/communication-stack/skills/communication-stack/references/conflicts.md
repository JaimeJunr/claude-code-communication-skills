# Where the communication recipes disagree

Apply the three-context ladder in [../SKILL.md](../SKILL.md). These ten rulings
reconcile the retained sources; they do not replace the user's output contract.

1. **T1 — Opening order.** Talk-normal and Rahul's answer-first rule wins. Give
   the requested answer, decision, or action before background or an optional
   analogy. Include a condition needed to interpret the answer. Audit: 436–471.
2. **T2 — Readable grammar.** Rahul's complete sentences and caveman's clarity
   principle win. Keep articles and grammatical relationships. Short labels
   are fine; ambiguous telegraph speech is not. Audit: 354–409.
3. **T3 — Caps versus substance.** Rahul's accuracy rule and ADHD's task-wins
   exception win. Required facts, risks, uncertainty, depth, and alternatives
   outrank word, sentence, option, or reading-time targets. Audit: 473–546,
   1053–1064.
4. **T4 — Ending and topic drift.** Talk-normal and conditional ADHD next
   actions win. End on useful content within the requested goal. No compulsory
   recap, secondary topic, or hypothetical offer. The same goal does not require
   repeating the opening. Audit: 611–683, 768–783.
5. **T5 — Repeated state.** Context-aware progress wins. Report changed state.
   During ongoing ADHD work, one useful step indicator or reorientation is
   allowed. Skip unchanged background and completed-task recaps. Audit:
   411–434, 914–932.
6. **T6 — List count.** ADHD completeness and Humanizer's genuine-item
   exception win. Keep real three-step procedures and every requested item.
   Group long lists without truncating or destroying meaningful hierarchy.
   Audit: 563–609.
7. **T7 — Author voice and detours.** No-ai-slop minimum edits and Humanizer
   sample matching win. Preserve intentional rhythm, humor, asides, uncertainty,
   and meaningful detours belonging to the requested piece. Chat topic control
   does not erase author voice. Audit: 742–783.
8. **T8 — Categorical bans.** Humanizer's contextual tests win. Keep useful
   qualifiers, technical actors, passive voice, informative contrasts, and
   sample-matched punctuation. Cut empty formulae instead of grammatical
   categories. Exact names and necessary technical senses survive word bans.
   Audit: 785–875, 1066–1079.
9. **T9 — Return contract.** The requested artifact contract and Humanizer's
   embedded mode win. Return final text once. Critique, drafts, and What changed
   require a request. Detect-only requests return findings without rewriting
   or guessing authorship. Audit: 961–979.
10. **T10 — Machine exactness.** Caveman's payload protection and STE's verbatim
    contract win. Never change protected code, commands, paths, errors, data,
    identifiers, or quotations to satisfy style preferences. Do not add comments
    during a communication-only rewrite. Existing commit payload stays exact;
    new engineering artifacts follow their task and project conventions.
    Audit: 527–561, 1003–1017, 1081–1108.
